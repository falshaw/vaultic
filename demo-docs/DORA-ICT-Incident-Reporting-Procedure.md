# ICT Incident Reporting and Classification Procedure

**Meridian Capital Partners Ltd** — *fictional firm, demonstration document only*
Document ref: MCP-PRO-INC-004 · Version 1.3 · Owner: Head of Compliance

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets out how the Firm classifies and reports ICT-related incidents
under DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554) and the related regulatory technical
standards.

## 2. Classification criteria

Each ICT-related incident is assessed against the following criteria:

- number and relevance of **clients and financial counterparts** affected;
- **duration and service downtime**;
- **geographical spread** of the impact;
- **data losses** affecting availability, authenticity, integrity, or confidentiality;
- **criticality of the services** affected;
- **economic impact**, both direct and indirect.

## 3. Major-incident thresholds (firm calibration)

An incident is treated as **major** where any of the following applies:

- more than **10% of active clients** are affected, or affected clients have a
  material impact on the Firm's reputation; or
- a critical or important service is **unavailable for more than 2 hours**; or
- there is any confirmed loss of data integrity or confidentiality on a critical
  service; or
- the incident has been escalated to the management body.

## 4. Reporting timelines for a major incident

When an incident is classified as major, the Firm notifies the competent
authority as follows:

- **Initial notification: within 4 hours of classification as major, and no later
  than 24 hours after the incident was detected.**
- **Intermediate report: within 72 hours** of the initial notification, updated
  whenever there is a material change in status.
- **Final report: within one month**, including root-cause analysis and remediation.

Significant cyber threats that have not (yet) materialised into an incident may be
reported to the competent authority on a **voluntary** basis.

## 5. Internal escalation

The on-call analyst raises a ticket and notifies the Incident Manager. For any
incident provisionally assessed as major, the Head of Compliance and the CISO are
notified **within 30 minutes**, and the management body is informed without undue
delay.
