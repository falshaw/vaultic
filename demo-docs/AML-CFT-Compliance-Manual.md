# AML/CFT Compliance Manual (excerpt)

**Meridian Capital Partners Ltd** — *fictional firm, demonstration document only*
Document ref: MCP-MAN-AML-002 · Version 4.0 · Owner: Money Laundering Reporting Officer

## 1. Customer due diligence

The Firm applies customer due diligence (CDD) **before establishing any business
relationship** and before carrying out an occasional transaction. CDD comprises
identification and verification of the customer, identification of the beneficial
owner, and an assessment of the purpose and intended nature of the relationship.

## 2. Enhanced due diligence

Enhanced due diligence (EDD) is applied to:

- politically exposed persons (PEPs), their family members and close associates;
- customers established in **high-risk third countries**; and
- any relationship or transaction presenting a higher risk of money laundering or
  terrorist financing.

EDD includes senior-management approval to onboard, establishing the source of
funds and source of wealth, and enhanced ongoing monitoring.

## 3. Reporting officer and internal escalation

The Firm appoints a **Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)**, who is the Head
of Compliance. Any transaction at or above **EUR 10,000** — or any transaction of
any value that appears unusual or lacks an apparent economic purpose — is escalated
to the MLRO **within 24 hours**. The MLRO decides whether to file a Suspicious
Activity Report (SAR) with the Financial Intelligence Unit.

## 4. Sanctions screening

All customers, beneficial owners, and counterparties are screened at onboarding
and continuously thereafter against the **EU consolidated list, OFAC SDN list, and
UN Security Council** sanctions lists. A positive match blocks the transaction and
is escalated to the MLRO immediately.

## 5. Record keeping

CDD records, transaction records, and SARs are retained for **at least five years**
after the end of the business relationship or the date of the occasional
transaction, and longer where required by national law.

## 6. Training

All staff complete AML/CFT training at onboarding and at least annually. Records of
completion are maintained by Compliance.
