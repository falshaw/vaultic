# ICT Risk Management Policy

**Meridian Capital Partners Ltd** — *fictional firm, demonstration document only*
Document ref: MCP-POL-ICT-001 · Version 2.1 · Owner: Chief Risk Officer

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy establishes the ICT risk management framework of Meridian Capital
Partners Ltd ("the Firm") in line with Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 on digital
operational resilience for the financial sector ("DORA"), which applies from
17 January 2025. It covers all ICT assets, systems, and third-party arrangements
that support the Firm's critical or important functions.

## 2. Governance

The management body bears ultimate responsibility for ICT risk. It approves this
policy, the digital operational resilience strategy, and the ICT risk tolerance,
and reviews them at least annually and after any major ICT-related incident.
ICT risk is a standing item on the quarterly Risk Committee agenda.

## 3. Recovery objectives for critical functions

For functions classified as critical or important, the Firm sets:

- **Recovery Time Objective (RTO): no more than 4 hours.**
- **Recovery Point Objective (RPO): no more than 15 minutes.**

Backups are taken continuously for transactional systems and tested by full
restore at least quarterly.

## 4. Register of Information

The Firm maintains a **Register of Information** covering all contractual
arrangements with ICT third-party service providers. The register records the
function supported, criticality, data location, sub-contracting chain, and
substitutability. It is **updated at least annually** and made available to the
competent authority on request. Arrangements supporting critical or important
functions are identified explicitly.

## 5. ICT third-party risk

Before entering any ICT contract, the Firm assesses concentration risk,
sub-outsourcing, data-location, and exit/termination rights. Contracts for
critical or important functions must include audit rights, security requirements,
incident-notification obligations, and a documented exit strategy.

## 6. Digital operational resilience testing

The Firm runs a risk-based testing programme including vulnerability scans,
network-security assessments, and scenario-based testing. Where the Firm is
designated significant, **threat-led penetration testing (TLPT) is performed at
least every three years** by an independent tester.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually, and immediately following a major
ICT-related incident or a material change to the Firm's ICT landscape.
